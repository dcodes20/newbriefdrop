Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Launch review: This policy describes the current app code. Before public launch, confirm your hosting provider’s data practices and Anthropic’s applicable data terms, and have this policy reviewed for your specific deployment.

BriefDrop (“BriefDrop,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by BriefDrop. We respect your privacy and explain below what information the current BriefDrop web app handles and how you can contact us.

Information the app handles

  • Brief preferences: Choices such as your craft, level, and topic may be kept temporarily in your browser session while you move through the app.
  • Saved and app state: Saved briefs, started-brief identifiers, brief history, cached briefs, and streak-freeze state are stored in your browser’s local storage. This information remains on that browser until you clear its site data or the app replaces it. It is not synced to an account.
  • Brief generation: When server-backed generation is enabled, your selected craft, level, topic, and brief titles used to avoid repeats are sent to our server and included in a request to Anthropic, our AI provider. Do not include sensitive or confidential information in a topic. Briefs can also be generated locally when the service is unavailable.
  • Account forms: The sign-up, sign-in, and password-reset screens in the current prototype are not connected to an account service. They do not create an account or send submitted form details to BriefDrop. Do not enter a real password or sensitive information into these prototype forms.
  • Technical information: The app code does not configure analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider or network infrastructure used to serve the site may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, and request time, in its ordinary operation. Confirm the deployed provider’s actual logging and retention practices before launch.

How information is used and shared

We use the information above to provide and maintain app features, generate practice briefs, remember your choices in your browser, and protect the service. When server-backed brief generation is used, Anthropic processes the generation request to provide its service. Anthropic’s handling of that request is also governed by its applicable terms and privacy practices. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process connection data to operate the site.

We do not sell personal information. We do not currently configure advertising or analytics services in the app. We do not control data practices of third-party providers; verify the providers and settings used in your deployment before launch.

Storage and retention

Browser storage is kept on the device and browser where you use BriefDrop. You can remove it using your browser’s site-data controls. Session storage is generally cleared when its browser tab or session ends. The current app does not provide account-based syncing or a server-side account deletion feature. Retention of requests by hosting or AI providers depends on their applicable settings and terms.

International processing

BriefDrop is operated from Nigeria. Hosting infrastructure and AI providers may process information in other countries. The locations and safeguards depend on the providers and deployment you choose; confirm these details before launch.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to use server-backed generation, avoid entering personal or confidential details in brief topics, and clear BriefDrop’s browser data using your browser settings. For privacy questions or requests, email briefdrop20@gmail.com. Because the current prototype does not maintain user accounts, we may need information from you to understand a request; please do not email passwords or sensitive identity documents.

Children

BriefDrop is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the service under the laws that apply to them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a deployed version of the service, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. We will update the date at the top when we publish a revised version. Check this page for the current text.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact BriefDrop at briefdrop20@gmail.com.
